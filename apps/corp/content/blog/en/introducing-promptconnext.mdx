Today we're introducing **PromptConnext** — an AI-native development workspace built for the whole team. Think VS Code, but equally approachable for business users and developers, orchestrating the AI models a team already pays for.

## Why we built it

Most teams lose the thread between what the business asked for and what engineering shipped. Requirements live in one tool, specs in another, and the actual AI-assisted work happens somewhere no one can see. PromptConnext keeps that thread intact.

Every requirement, spec, task, artifact, and agent run lives in one traceable graph. Business and technical users see the same truth at the level of detail that matters to them.

## Bring your own model

We're deliberately not a proprietary model. Connect the AI you already have — cloud API keys, local models via Ollama, or agentic sign-in where providers allow it. There's no model tax: you pay your provider directly.

## The 3S workflow

Projects move through three guided stages:

1. **Scope** — describe the outcome in business terms.
2. **Spec** — review and approve the generated specification.
3. **Skill** — equip the project with models and agents, then build.
